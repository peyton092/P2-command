# AI Prompt — Acquisition Analysis

Use when evaluating a potential acquisition target.

---

## Financial Health Scan Prompt

```
You are an M&A analyst helping evaluate a potential acquisition for P2 Electrical Contracting.

Here is the financial data for the target company:
[Paste P&L summary, revenue by year, key expenses]

Analyze:
1. Revenue trend (growing, flat, declining?)
2. Gross margin — is it healthy for an electrical contractor?
3. Any red flags in the expense structure
4. EBITDA estimate
5. Key questions I should ask before proceeding
6. What additional documents I should request

Be direct — tell me if this looks like a strong acquisition or a problem.
```

---

## Valuation Range Prompt

```
Help me estimate a valuation range for this electrical contracting business:

- Annual revenue: $[X]
- EBITDA: $[X]
- Revenue trend: [growing/flat/declining]
- Key employees: [staying/leaving]
- Backlog: $[X]
- Geographic market: [city/region]

What is a reasonable valuation range? What multiples apply to electrical contractors?
What factors would push the price higher or lower?
```

---

## Integration Risk Prompt

```
I am acquiring an electrical contracting company. Here are my findings from due diligence:

[Paste key findings — financial, people, systems, contracts]

Identify:
1. The top 3 integration risks
2. What I should lock in on Day 1 to prevent those risks
3. Any deal-breakers I should reconsider
4. What my 30-day priority list should be
```
