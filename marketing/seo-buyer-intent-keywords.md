## Filters applied

- **Excluded:** "how to", "DIY", "what is", "cost calculator only", national informational queries
- **Included:** "near me", `[CITY]`, "emergency", "same day", "[problem] + repair/installation", "licensed/24 hour"
- **Difficulty:** 1 = easy to rank in <30 days with GBP + on-page + 3-5 citations; 10 = national authority required
- **Revenue potential:** weighted by avg. ticket × close rate × monthly search volume

## 30-day execution order

1. **Week 1** — GBP fully optimized, 5 service categories, 20 photos, weekly posts. Publish or upgrade the homepage geo block targeting `electrician [CITY]` and `electrician near me`.
2. **Week 1–2** — Ship Tier 1 emergency + panel pages (#1–#10). These convert fastest.
3. **Week 2–3** — Ship Tier 4 neighborhood pages (#29–#32) for your top 5 suburbs. Lowest competition, fastest ranking.
4. **Week 3–4** — Ship Tier 2 high-ticket install pages (#11–#20). Bias toward EV chargers and generators (highest ticket, growing demand).
5. **Ongoing** — Tier 3 commercial pages once Tier 1 is converting.

## What to skip

- Generic "best electrician" — informational, no buyer intent
- "How much does an electrician cost" without geo — research stage
- "Electrician salary" — job-seeker traffic
- "DIY [anything]" — opposite of buyer intent
- Broad "electrical services" with no geo modifier — too competitive, low conversion
