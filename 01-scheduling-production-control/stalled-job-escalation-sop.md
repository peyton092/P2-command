# Stalled Job Escalation SOP

A job is "stalled" when it has not progressed to the next phase within 5 business days of its planned date.

---

## Escalation Levels

### Level 1 — PM Response (Days 1-2 behind)
- PM contacts foreman to identify root cause
- Root cause documented in job phase tracker
- Recovery plan created with new milestone dates
- GC notified if their delay is the cause (use GC Delay Notice template)

### Level 2 — Owner Notified (Days 3-5 behind)
- PM briefs owner on status, cause, and recovery plan
- Owner reviews if crew redeployment is needed
- Billing reviewed — is anything billable despite the stall?
- GC relationship call made by Owner or PM

### Level 3 — Crisis Mode (5+ days behind)
- Daily check-in on this job until resolved
- Crew redeployment or overtime authorized by Owner
- Formal delay documentation sent to GC
- Legal/contract review if GC is causing delay and contract has penalties

---

## Root Cause Categories
- [ ] GC delay (other trades, access, decisions)
- [ ] Material shortage (identify item and supplier)
- [ ] Crew shortage (sickness, no-show, understaffed)
- [ ] Inspection failure (document what failed)
- [ ] Weather (document dates)
- [ ] Scope change / design issue
- [ ] P2 scheduling error
