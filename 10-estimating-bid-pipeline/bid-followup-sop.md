# Bid Follow-Up SOP

Bids don't win themselves. The estimate gets us in the door — the follow-up wins the job. This SOP defines exactly when and how we chase every bid after it's sent.

---

## The Follow-Up Clock

The clock starts the day a bid moves to **Sent**. Log every touch in the Bid Tracker's **Last Follow-Up** column and set the **Next Follow-Up** date.

| Touch | Timing After Sent | Method | Goal |
|-------|-------------------|--------|------|
| #1 — Confirm receipt | Same day or next morning | Email or call | "Confirming you received our proposal — happy to walk through it." |
| #2 — Check-in | 3 business days | Call preferred | Answer questions, confirm scope is understood |
| #3 — Status | 7 business days | Call | "Where does our bid stand? Anything you need from us?" |
| #4 — Decision push | 14 days | Call | "Are we still in the running? When do you expect to award?" |
| Ongoing | Every 7–10 days | Call/email | Until we get a Won / Lost decision |

> Stop following up only when the bid is marked **Won**, **Lost**, or the customer explicitly tells us the project is dead.

---

## Follow-Up Standards

- **Call first, email second.** A phone call gets a real answer; email gets ignored.
- **Always add value.** Don't just "check in" — offer a value-engineering idea, confirm a long-lead item, or ask a smart scope question.
- **Log it or it didn't happen.** Every touch updates the tracker. No mental notes.
- **Never let a sent bid go dark.** A bid with no Next Follow-Up date is a red flag.

---

## When We Win

1. Update the bid Stage to **Won** and record the decision date.
2. Open a Job # and hand the file to the assigned PM.
3. Move the row to **Decided — Last 90 Days** with the converted Job #.
4. Confirm the contract/PO matches our bid scope and number before mobilizing.

---

## When We Lose

1. Update the bid Stage to **Lost** and record the decision date.
2. **Ask why.** Make the call — price, schedule, relationship, scope? Get the real reason.
3. Log the reason in `win-loss-log.md`.
4. If we lost on price, note who won and by roughly how much — that's market intel.

---

## Red Flags

| Flag | Action |
|------|--------|
| Sent bid with no Next Follow-Up date | Set one today |
| Bid 14+ days sent with no decision and no recent contact | Estimator works it today; escalate to Owner if no response |
| Hit rate drops below target | Owner + Estimator review pricing and follow-up discipline |
| Repeatedly losing to the same competitor | Competitive review at executive meeting |
