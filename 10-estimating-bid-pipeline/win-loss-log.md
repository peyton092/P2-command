# Win/Loss Log

Every decided bid gets logged here with the real reason. This is how we learn what wins work and where we're leaving money — or losing it. Reviewed monthly by Owner + Estimator.

---

## Reason Codes

Use these consistently so we can spot patterns.

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Price** | We were too high (or won on price) |
| **Schedule** | Couldn't meet timeline, or our availability won/lost it |
| **Relationship** | Existing relationship with us or a competitor drove the call |
| **Scope** | Scope mismatch, missed items, or superior scope coverage |
| **Capacity** | We chose not to / couldn't take it on (drives most No-Bids) |
| **Other** | Anything else — explain in Lesson/Action |

---

## Monthly Review Questions

1. **Hit rate:** Won ÷ (Won + Lost) for the month. Trending up or down?
2. **Where are we losing?** Most common loss reason this month?
3. **Price gaps:** When we lost on price, how far off were we? Are we leaving margin on the table when we win?
4. **No-bids:** What did we walk away from, and was that the right call?
5. **One change:** What's the single adjustment to pricing or follow-up for next month?
