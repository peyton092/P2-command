## Bid Stages

Every bid moves through these stages. The **Stage** column on the board below always reflects the latest one reached.

| Stage | Means | Next Action |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| **Lead** | Invitation to bid / opportunity identified — not yet decided to pursue | Bid / No-Bid decision |
| **Pricing** | We've committed to bid; takeoff and pricing in progress | Finish numbers |
| **Priced** | Numbers are done and reviewed — not yet packaged | Build the proposal |
| **Made** | Bid/proposal packaged and ready to send | Send it |
| **Sent** | Submitted to GC / customer | Start follow-up clock |
| **Followed Up** | At least one follow-up contact made after sending | Keep following up until decision |
| **Won** | Awarded to P2 — convert to a job | Open Job #, hand to PM |
| **Lost** | Awarded to someone else, or customer declined | Log reason in Win/Loss Log |
| **No-Bid** | We declined to bid | Log reason in Win/Loss Log |

## Rules

1. Every opportunity gets a Bid # the day it comes in — even if we No-Bid it later.
2. A bid is never "Sent" without a Next Follow-Up date. (See `bid-followup-sop.md`.)
3. Every Won bid converts to a Job # before any work or ordering starts.
4. Every Lost and No-Bid gets a reason logged in `win-loss-log.md` — we learn from both.
5. The board is reviewed every Monday. Stale bids (no movement in 14 days) get escalated to the Owner.
