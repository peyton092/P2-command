# Job Status Definitions

Everyone uses the same language. No ambiguity.

---

## Status: ON TRACK
- All phase milestones within 2 days of planned dates
- Billing current through last completed phase
- No open inspection failures
- Foreman reports being submitted

## Status: AT RISK
- 3–5 days behind on any milestone
- A billing gap exists (phase complete but not invoiced)
- An inspection failure has not been re-scheduled within 48 hours
- GC delay has not been formally documented
- **Action required:** PM creates recovery plan within 24 hours

## Status: BEHIND
- 6+ days behind on any milestone
- More than one phase completed without billing
- Re-inspection has not been scheduled
- **Action required:** Owner notified, recovery plan active, daily check-in

## Status: ON HOLD
- Job paused due to GC, weather, permit, or design issue
- P2 is not the cause of the hold
- Documented in writing
- Crew redeployed to another job
- **Action required:** Formal hold notice sent to GC, billing reviewed for what can be invoiced now

## Status: COMPLETE
- Final inspection passed
- Punch list signed off
- Final invoice sent
- Retention request submitted (if applicable)
- CompanyCam fully documented

## Status: CLOSED
- All invoices paid
- Retention received
- Job file archived

---

## Phase Definitions

| Phase | Definition |
|-------|-----------|
| Pre-Construction | Permit pulled, materials ordered, schedule set |
| Underground / Slab | All below-grade or in-slab conduit/wire |
| Rough-In | All in-wall wiring, boxes, panel rough — prior to drywall |
| Service | Meter, service entrance, main panel permanent set |
| Trim | Devices, fixtures, covers, panel trim |
| Final | All work complete, inspection pending or passed |
