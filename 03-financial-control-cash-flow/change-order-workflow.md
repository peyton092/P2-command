# Change Order Workflow

Every dollar of extra work follows this process. No exceptions.

---

## Step 1 — Identify in Field
- Foreman identifies work outside original scope
- Foreman documents: what, where, why, and photos in CompanyCam (ISSUES album)
- Foreman notifies PM **same day** — verbal first, then text/email

## Step 2 — Price It
- PM prices the change order within 24 hours
- Pricing includes: labor hours + material + markup
- Use standard markup: **[INSERT YOUR MARKUP %]**
- CO prepared using Change Order form (see 06-templates)

## Step 3 — Get Written Approval
- CO sent to GC or owner via email — written only, no verbal approvals
- GC has 48 hours to respond before work is paused
- If GC delays approval, PM documents the delay (protects P2)
- **No written approval = No work performed on that scope**

## Step 4 — Bill It
- Once approved, CO is logged in job profitability tracker
- Invoice generated within 48 hours of written approval
- CO tracked in weekly billing audit

## Rules That Protect Margin
1. Never start CO work without written approval — not even "they said go ahead."
2. If a GC tells you verbally to proceed, send a confirming email before starting: "Confirming you've approved [CO description] for $[amount] — proceeding per your direction."
3. All COs priced at full margin — no discounting extra work.
4. Disputed COs escalate to Owner within 24 hours — do not let them go stale.
