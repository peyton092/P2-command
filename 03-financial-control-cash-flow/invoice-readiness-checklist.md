# Invoice Readiness Checklist

Run this before generating any invoice. Takes 2 minutes. Prevents disputes.

---

## For Every Invoice

- [ ] Phase or milestone confirmed complete (who confirmed: _________)
- [ ] Inspection passed (if billing on inspection trigger) — record result
- [ ] CompanyCam photos uploaded and current
- [ ] Foreman daily report submitted for the completion date
- [ ] Invoice amount matches schedule of values (or approved CO amount)
- [ ] Invoice sent to correct contact at GC / Owner (confirm email on file)
- [ ] Invoice references correct job name, address, and PO# (if required)
- [ ] Payment terms stated on invoice (net 30 is standard — confirm contract)

---

## For Change Order Invoices

- [ ] Written approval from GC on file (email or signed form)
- [ ] CO amount matches what was approved
- [ ] CO number matches tracking log

---

## For Final Invoices

- [ ] Final inspection certificate on file
- [ ] Punch list fully closed and signed off
- [ ] All prior invoices paid or addressed
- [ ] Retention amount clearly stated and tracked separately

---

## After Sending
- [ ] Invoice logged in AR tracker with due date
- [ ] Follow-up reminder set for 5 days before due date
- [ ] If unpaid at due date — call (not email) within 24 hours
