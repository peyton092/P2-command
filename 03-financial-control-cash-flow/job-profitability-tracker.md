## Margin Killers to Watch
1. **Unpriced change orders** — extra work performed without a CO
2. **Re-inspections** — failed inspections cost crew time + delay billing
3. **Material waste** — over-ordering or theft
4. **Crew overtime** — schedule slippage that forces OT to catch up
5. **Callbacks** — warranty/quality issues after job closes
