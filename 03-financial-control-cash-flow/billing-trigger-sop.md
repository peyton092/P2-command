# Billing Trigger SOP

Cash moves when work is complete. These are the non-negotiable billing triggers.

---

## Trigger 1 — Rough-In Complete
**Trigger:** Rough-in inspection passed (photo of pass in CompanyCam)
**Who identifies:** Foreman
**Who invoices:** Office
**Timeline:** Invoice sent within 24 hours of inspection pass
**Amount:** Per contract schedule of values — rough-in percentage

## Trigger 2 — Trim Complete
**Trigger:** Trim inspection passed OR trim-out checklist signed off
**Who identifies:** Foreman + PM confirmation
**Who invoices:** Office
**Timeline:** Invoice sent within 24 hours
**Amount:** Per contract schedule of values — trim percentage

## Trigger 3 — Final Complete
**Trigger:** Final inspection passed, punch list closed
**Who identifies:** PM
**Who invoices:** Office — with PM authorization
**Timeline:** Invoice sent same day as final pass
**Amount:** Remaining contract balance minus retention

## Trigger 4 — Change Order Approved
**Trigger:** Written GC approval received (email or signed CO form)
**Who identifies:** PM or Foreman
**Who invoices:** Office
**Timeline:** Invoice sent within 48 hours of written approval
**Amount:** Full CO amount

## Trigger 5 — Retention Release
**Trigger:** Project substantially complete, GC releases retention
**Who identifies:** PM
**Who invoices:** Office
**Timeline:** Request sent within 5 days of final CO / substantial completion
**Amount:** Full retention amount

---

## The Rule
**No completed phase sits unbilled for more than 48 hours.**
If it does, it is a billing failure — not a scheduling issue.

---

## Weekly Billing Audit
Every Wednesday, Office runs through active jobs and confirms:
- [ ] All passed inspections have been invoiced
- [ ] All approved COs have been invoiced
- [ ] No job is more than one phase ahead without billing the prior phase
