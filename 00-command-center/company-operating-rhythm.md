# Company Operating Rhythm

## Daily (Every Day)
| Time | Action | Owner |
|------|--------|-------|
| 6:00 AM | Dispatch confirmed — crews have assignments | Office / PM |
| 6:30 AM | Foremen depart with materials staged | Foreman |
| 4:00 PM | Foreman daily report submitted | Foreman |
| 4:30 PM | PM reviews reports, flags issues | PM |
| 5:00 PM | Tomorrow's dispatch locked | Office |

## Weekly
| Day | Action | Owner |
|-----|--------|-------|
| Monday 8 AM | Executive review — jobs, cash, problems | Owner + PM |
| Monday 9 AM | Schedule locked for the week | PM |
| Wednesday | Mid-week billing push — all completed phases invoiced | Office |
| Friday 3 PM | Weekly KPI scoreboard posted | PM |
| Friday 4 PM | Next week lookahead confirmed | PM |

## Monthly
- Full job profitability review (all active jobs)
- Foreman scorecards issued
- Cash flow projection updated (90-day rolling)
- Acquisition pipeline reviewed

## Quarterly
- Full P&L review with accountant
- Overhead vs. revenue analysis
- Key hire / key fire decisions
- Strategic target list updated (acquisitions)
