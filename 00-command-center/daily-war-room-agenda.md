# Daily War Room Agenda

**Duration:** 15 minutes max. No exceptions.
**Time:** 7:00 AM (or immediately after crew dispatch)
**Attendees:** Owner, PM, Office Lead

---

## Agenda (Strict Order)

### 1. Crews Deployed (2 min)
- How many crews out today?
- Any no-shows or gaps?
- Coverage issues flagged immediately.

### 2. Job Status (5 min)
- Any job at risk of missing milestone today?
- Any inspection scheduled — is it ready?
- Any material shortages blocking work?

### 3. Money (3 min)
- What got billed yesterday?
- What payments came in?
- Any change orders approved/pending?

### 4. Problems (3 min)
- What is on fire right now?
- Who owns the fix and by when?

### 5. Tomorrow (2 min)
- Any special setup needed for tomorrow?
- Materials to order today?

---

## Rules
- No phones except to pull up job info.
- Every problem gets an OWNER and a DEADLINE — not a discussion.
- If it takes more than 15 minutes, schedule a separate meeting.
