# Weekly Executive Review

**When:** Every Monday, 8:00 AM
**Duration:** 45 minutes
**Attendees:** Owner, PM, Office/Finance Lead

---

## Section 1 — Job Health (15 min)

For every active job, answer:
- [ ] On schedule or behind? (days behind?)
- [ ] Budget on track?
- [ ] Any inspections failed or pending?
- [ ] Billing current through last completed phase?

**Action:** Flag any job that is behind schedule OR behind on billing. Assign owner to fix within 48 hours.

---

## Section 2 — Cash Flow (10 min)

- Revenue billed this week: $___________
- Cash collected this week: $___________
- Outstanding AR (30/60/90 day buckets): $___________
- Pending change orders not yet billed: $___________
- Payroll this week: $___________
- Net cash position: $___________

**Action:** Any AR over 45 days gets a phone call this week — not an email.

---

## Section 3 — People (10 min)

- Any foreman performance issues?
- Any crew problems (safety, no-shows, quality)?
- Any hiring needs?
- Anyone ready for promotion?

---

## Section 4 — Upcoming (10 min)

- New jobs starting this week — are they set up?
- Inspections scheduled — crews ready?
- Material orders needed?
- Any GC relationship issues?

---

## Output
Every meeting produces:
1. A written list of action items with owners and deadlines.
2. Updated job status in master schedule.
3. Any billing that got missed — invoiced before end of day Monday.
