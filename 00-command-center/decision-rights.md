# Decision Rights

Who can decide what — without asking permission.

---

## Owner (Peyton)
- Any contract over $50,000
- Hiring or firing any employee
- Any acquisition or partnership
- Any change to company pricing or margin targets
- Any debt or financing decision
- Vendor agreements over $10,000/year

---

## Project Manager
- Job scheduling changes
- Crew assignments and redeployment
- Material orders up to $5,000 per order
- Change order initiation (pricing must be approved by Owner if over $10,000)
- Foreman performance warnings
- Subcontractor coordination

---

## Office / Finance Lead
- Invoice generation and sending
- AR follow-up calls
- Vendor payments under $2,500
- Payroll processing
- Scheduling inspections

---

## Foreman
- Daily crew task assignment within a job
- Minor material requests (approved list)
- Safety stops — a foreman can halt work for safety with NO approval needed
- Photo documentation decisions

---

## Escalation Rule
If you are unsure whether a decision is yours to make — **stop and ask within 1 hour**.
Do not delay work waiting for approval. Flag it, keep working on other tasks, get the answer.

---

## What Gets People Fired
- Making a financial commitment without authority
- Hiding a problem instead of escalating
- Skipping safety stops
- Signing anything on behalf of P2 without Owner sign-off
