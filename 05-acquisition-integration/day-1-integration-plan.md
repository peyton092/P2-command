# Day 1 Integration Plan

The first 24 hours after close set the tone. Execute this exactly.

---

## Before Close (Final Week)

- [ ] Employment agreements prepared for key employees to sign Day 1
- [ ] Bank accounts and payment information ready to transfer
- [ ] P2 insurance added to cover acquired entity
- [ ] IT / system access prepared
- [ ] Communication plan drafted (what are you telling employees, GCs, vendors?)
- [ ] Physical signage / branding plan decided (immediate or phased?)

---

## Hour 0-2 — Leadership Meeting

**Who:** Owner (Peyton) + acquired company owner/principals + key managers
**Agenda:**
1. Welcome and vision (5 min) — why this happened and what it means
2. What stays the same (5 min) — day-to-day operations continue
3. What changes immediately (10 min) — reporting, billing, communications
4. Key contacts at P2 (5 min) — who to call for what
5. Q&A (10 min)

**Do NOT lead with:** org changes, layoffs, rebrand — do not destabilize on Day 1.

---

## Hour 2-4 — Employee Communication

- All-hands meeting or small group meetings with every employee
- Message: "You have jobs, you have paychecks, here is your point of contact"
- Distribute P2 contact sheet
- Collect any immediate concerns

---

## Hour 4-8 — Operational Handoff

- [ ] Keys, codes, access transferred
- [ ] Bank account access transferred / new signers added
- [ ] All active jobs listed and reviewed with PM
- [ ] Foremen introduced to P2 reporting requirements
- [ ] CompanyCam set up for acquired jobs
- [ ] Pending invoices and AR reviewed

---

## End of Day 1

- [ ] Every employee knows who their manager is
- [ ] Every active job has a P2 PM assigned
- [ ] Payroll confirmed for the next cycle
- [ ] No billing has stopped
- [ ] No crew has been left without direction

---

## Day 1 — What NOT to Do
- Do not announce layoffs or org changes
- Do not change compensation on Day 1
- Do not rebrand vehicles or sites immediately without a plan
- Do not cancel vendor relationships without review
- Do not let employees go home without knowing who to call tomorrow
