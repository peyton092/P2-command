# Acquisition Due Diligence Checklist

Do not make an offer until every section is complete.

## Section 1 — Financial Health

- [ ] Last 3 years P&L statements reviewed
- [ ] Last 3 years tax returns reviewed
- [ ] Current YTD financials reviewed
- [ ] Gross margin by job type analyzed
- [ ] AR aging schedule reviewed (how much is collectible?)
- [ ] AP schedule reviewed (what debts transfer?)
- [ ] Bank statements last 6 months reviewed
- [ ] Any liens filed against the company?
- [ ] Any judgments or lawsuits active?
- [ ] Bonding capacity and current bonds reviewed
- [ ] Insurance (GL, workers comp) reviewed — any open claims?
- [ ] Equipment list and condition reviewed
- [ ] Vehicle list, ownership, and loans reviewed

## Section 2 — Key Employees

- [ ] Org chart obtained
- [ ] Owner / principals identified — are they staying or leaving?
- [ ] Key foremen identified — would they stay post-acquisition?
- [ ] Office / admin staff identified
- [ ] Any non-compete agreements in place?
- [ ] Employment agreements reviewed
- [ ] Compensation structure documented
- [ ] Any union affiliation?

## Section 3 — Contracts and Work

- [ ] All active contracts reviewed
- [ ] Backlog calculated: $
- [ ] Contract terms reviewed (payment terms, retentions, bonding requirements)
- [ ] Any contracts that require novation / approval of assignment?
- [ ] GC relationship quality assessed (talk to 2-3 GC contacts)
- [ ] Any contracts in dispute?
- [ ] Warranty obligations outstanding?
- [ ] License — is it transferable? In whose name?

---

## Section 4 — Operations

- [ ] What software / systems do they use?
- [ ] How do they dispatch?
- [ ] How do they track jobs?
- [ ] How do they bill?
- [ ] What is their safety record? (EMR rate)
- [ ] Any OSHA citations in last 3 years?
- [ ] Vehicle / equipment condition inspected in person
