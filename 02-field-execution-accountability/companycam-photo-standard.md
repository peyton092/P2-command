# CompanyCam Photo Standard

Every job in CompanyCam follows this exact structure. No improvising.

---

## Required Photo Albums (in order)

### 1. BEFORE
- Site as-found conditions
- Existing panels, wiring, infrastructure
- Any pre-existing damage documented
- Take before ANY work begins

### 2. ROUGH-IN
- All rough-in wiring runs (labeled by circuit if complex)
- Panel rough-in with breakers identified
- Box placement (every room, every location)
- Underground conduit before backfill
- Slab rough before pour
- Inspector present at inspection (photo of inspector + board if possible)
- Rough-in inspection result (pass/fail tag or paperwork)

### 3. TRIM
- All device locations installed
- Panel trim-out (full panel photo + directory)
- Fixture installations
- Any custom or non-standard installations

### 4. ISSUES
- Every deficiency, damage, or problem found
- Every GC delay (date-stamped photo of the blocking condition)
- Any failed inspection item
- Any unsafe condition found on site

### 5. FINAL
- Full walkthrough photos at completion
- Panel with directory complete
- Certificate of Occupancy / Final inspection paperwork
- Any owner/GC sign-off documentation

---

## Rules
- Every photo gets a **caption** — location + what it shows
- Photos uploaded **same day** — not at end of week
- If CompanyCam is down, photos go to the job's Google Drive folder
- Foreman is responsible. If photos aren't there, the foreman is responsible.

---

## What Photos Protect You From
- GC claiming damage was caused by P2
- Disputes about what was installed
- Failed inspections with no documentation
- Change order disputes ("we never asked for that")
